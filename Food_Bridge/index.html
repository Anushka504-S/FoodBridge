About FoodBridge

FoodBridge aims to connect surplus food with people who need it through donors, NGOs and authorized distribution partners.

How FoodBridge Works

1. Donate

Donors share details about available surplus food.

2. Connect

NGOs review donations and arrange collection.

3. Deliver

Suitable food is safely distributed to people in need.
